The Deed of Assignment

On 11 September 2026 Nelson Consulting, Inc. and Walt Nelson (together the Assignor) assigned to Wayne Stewart (the Assignee), without payment, all right, title and interest in Foundation in every version, including source code, forms, resources and documentation, together with the names "Foundation" and "Foundation Shell", the goodwill attaching to them, and the domain name foundationshell.com.

The Assignor warranted that it was the sole owner of the rights assigned, that it had not assigned, exclusively licensed or otherwise encumbered them, and that every licence previously granted to its customers is non-exclusive.

Nothing in the deed restricts the Assignor, or any existing customer of the Assignor, from continuing to use the software.

Conditions of the assignment

The Assignee undertook to:

  1. Release the software under an OSI-approved permissive licence (MIT or Apache 2.0) by 1 December 2026. The MIT licence was chosen.
  2. Permanently retain the names of Dave Batton and Walt Nelson in the credits, About box and documentation as the authors of the versions each wrote, and keep the modification history intact.
  3. Not sell the software or the rights assigned.

Condition 2 is why the Created by and Modified by lines in method headers, and the chronology in aaaMods, are treated as load-bearing and are never removed by any automated sweep. Contributors are asked to preserve them.

The signed instrument

The signed deed is held privately by Wayne Stewart and is not committed to this repository. It is available on request to anyone with a legitimate need to verify title, for example a downstream adopter's legal review.

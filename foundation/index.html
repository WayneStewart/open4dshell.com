Foundation

Foundation is an application shell component for 4D: it supplies the menus, windows, forms, find, sort, preferences and other parts of an application around your own tables and forms.

Foundation 6.0.0-beta.1

Foundation 6.0.0-beta.1 is a private beta from 1 November 2026. The code is already under the MIT licence, there is nothing to sign, and the beta is free.

To join, email your GitHub username to the contact address. You will be given Read access to github.com/open4dshell/Foundation-Shell, which is private until 1 December 2026, when it becomes public. Report problems in that repository's Issues.

Getting started lists the installation steps.

Requirements

  • 4D 20 LTS or later, on macOS or Windows.
  • Builds for 20 LTS, 20 R10, 21 LTS and 21 R, each as a notarised DMG for macOS and a ZIP for Windows.

Demo

Foundation Product Sales is a demonstration database built on Foundation. It is public at github.com/open4dshell/Foundation-Product-Sales.

History

Dave Batton created Foundation in 1993. Walt Nelson / Nelson Consulting, Inc. carried it from 2008, including Foundation 5 for 4D v11. On 11 September 2026 it was assigned to Wayne Stewart by deed for release under the MIT licence, with the credit to Dave Batton and Walt Nelson kept permanently.

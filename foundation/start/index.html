Getting started

You need 4D 20 LTS or later, on macOS or Windows. The release files and the documents linked below are in open4dshell/Foundation-Shell, which is private until 1 December 2026: during the beta you need Read access, which you get by emailing your GitHub username to the contact address.

  1. Download the DMG (macOS) or ZIP (Windows) for your 4D from the repository's Releases: 20L for 20 LTS, 20R for 20 R10, 21L for 21 LTS, 21R for 21 R. All are 6.0.0-beta.1 from the same commit. The SHA-256 checksums are in Release/releases.md.
  2. Install: copy Foundation.4dbase into your project's Components/ folder and reopen the project. For development, the repository itself also works as an interpreted component.
  3. New host? Clone Foundation Product Sales (public) and read its Fnd_Hook_* and Fnd_Host_* methods: they are the whole contract between a host and Foundation. Copy the ones you need.
  4. Foundation 5 host? Read Release/UPGRADING.md first. The breaking changes are few and listed: Foundation's own licensing methods are gone, cs.fnd.wnd is no longer published, the Logging component is no longer required, and German is DE, not GR.
  5. Problems: open an Issue in Foundation-Shell, giving the 4D version and the target you used. Compile warnings are not bugs; errors and misbehaviour are.
